Titan Orbit

titanorbit.io · October 12, 2026

Titan Orbit is a multiplayer space game played in the browser. This page describes the game and why it asks for a Google account. You can read it without signing in.

Play Titan Orbit

What the game does

You fly a ship, mine, and fight with other players in the same match. The game itself is at https://titanorbit.io/.

You can play as a guest, create a username and password, or sign in with Google when that option is offered. A name you type in the game is shown to other players. While you play, the game server receives your ship controls, position, and match actions so other players see the same match.

Why Titan Orbit requests Google account data

Sign in with Google is optional. If you choose it, Google gives Titan Orbit your Google account ID, your name, and your email address. We use those only to:

  • sign you into the game and recognize you on a later visit
  • keep your game progress and Orbit Unlocked purchase on that same player
  • reply if you ask about your account

We store that identity with Unity Authentication, tied to a Titan Orbit player ID. We do not sell Google user data. We do not use your Google account data to choose ads or to build an advertising profile. Advertising in the free game is separate and is covered in the privacy policy.

The full explanation is in the privacy policy at https://titanorbit.io/privacy. That is the same privacy policy linked from the Google sign-in screen.

Orbit Unlocked

The free game shows Google ads. Orbit Unlocked is a one-time purchase for the signed-in player. It removes those ads and unlocks the full hangar cosmetics. Stripe processes the payment. The unlock stays on that player.
