Privacy policy

Titan Orbit · titanorbit.io · October 11, 2026

Titan Orbit is a multiplayer space game played in the browser. This page explains what information the game uses and why. If you sign in with Google, read the Google account section below. That is the data Google shares with this game.

Who we are

The operator of titanorbit.io runs Titan Orbit. Questions, data requests, and deletion requests go to the user support email shown for Titan Orbit on the Google sign-in screen.

Google account data

If you choose Sign in with Google, Google gives Titan Orbit your Google account ID, your name, and your email address. We use those only to:

  • sign you into the game and recognize you on a later visit
  • keep your game progress and Orbit Unlocked purchase on that same player
  • reply if you ask about your account

We store that identity with Unity Authentication, tied to a Titan Orbit player ID. We do not sell Google user data. We do not use your Google account data to choose ads or to build an advertising profile. We do not share it with anyone except the service providers listed below, and only so they can run sign-in, saved progress, and purchases.

Account and saved progress

The game also supports a guest session and a username with a password. Unity Authentication stores the player ID and, when you create one, the username. The password is stored by Unity as a credential. We do not keep a copy of the password in the game.

Unity Cloud Save stores whether this player owns Orbit Unlocked. The browser keeps a sign-in session in local storage so you stay signed in on that device until you sign out or clear the site data.

A name you type in the game is shown to other players.

Purchases

Orbit Unlocked is bought through Stripe Checkout. Stripe collects the payment details and the email address on the receipt. We send Stripe your player ID so the unlock can be applied to that account. Stripe’s own privacy policy covers the payment. We store the result as “this player owns Orbit Unlocked,” not your card number.

Advertising

The free game shows Google ads, including rewarded video ads (Google AdSense). Orbit Unlocked removes those ads. Google may use cookies and similar identifiers to deliver and measure ads under Google’s policies. That advertising data is separate from the Google account data we receive when you sign in.

Gameplay

While you play, the game server receives your ship controls, position, and match actions so other players can see the same match. That traffic is tied to your player session.

Service providers

  • Google — Sign in with Google, and ads in the free game
  • Unity — player accounts and Cloud Save
  • Stripe — checkout for Orbit Unlocked
  • Cloudflare — hosting for titanorbit.io

How long we keep it

We keep account and purchase records for as long as the account exists. A sign-in session in the browser lasts until you sign out or clear site data. You can ask us to delete the account data we hold. Deletion removes the Titan Orbit player and the saved unlock tied to it. Google, Stripe, and Unity may keep their own records under their policies.

Children

Titan Orbit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child gave us personal information, contact the support email and we will delete it.
